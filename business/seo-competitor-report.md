# Eastbridge: SEO and AI-visibility competitor report

Baseline date: 31 October 2026. Prepared for Philip Chapman. Research only: no site code was changed.

---

## 1. Summary

1. **Nobody owns the client-side villa niche in search yet.** For 15 of the 20 target queries, position 1 goes to a portal, a news site, a job board or a contractor. No specialist firm holds a strong top spot. The openings are real.
2. **AI-style answers already cite three of our named competitors.** When a target query is asked as a question ("do I need a project manager to build a villa in Dubai?", "owner's representative Dubai"), the answer cites **DNA Project Management, PRASOON and Tanmu PMS**. These are the firms to beat for AI citations, and none of their pages is strong: DNA's article has no byline, no sources and no FAQ.
3. **Approval content is won by permit specialists.** Dar Al Naseeb has published 92 approval guides since January 2026, many of them on the same day. It and other permit firms (dubaiapprovals.com, buildingapprovals.ae, tarasukh.com, firstunicorninteriors.com) dominate "Nakheel NOC" and "renovation NOC" queries. We should not try to out-volume them. We should write the owner's version: what the approval sequence does to the programme and the budget.
4. **Cost content is crowded but beatable.** Engel & Völkers ranks first for "villa construction cost per square foot dubai". The rest of the results are contractor blogs with contradictory numbers. Our cost article, with every figure sourced, is the right kind of asset. What it needs now is links and citations from other sites.
5. **Eastbridge's domain is not live yet.** eastbridgestrategicpartners.com returned no response on 31 Oct 2026, so our position is "-" for every query. Getting the site live and indexed is the single biggest lever, ahead of any new content.
6. **Two things set us apart and no competitor has either:** (a) 21 years on the builder's side of the table, and (b) careful legal wording under Dubai Law No. 14 of 2025. At least one competitor's homepage says it will "supervise every supplier". That is the kind of wording the law reserves for DM-registered consultancies. Our compliant wording is a trust signal, not a weakness.

> **Method caveat.** These results come from the research tool's web index, which is US-based, plus fetches of the competitor sites. They are not a Google UAE results page. Positions are therefore a directional baseline. Once the site is live, confirm them in Google Search Console and with a UAE-located incognito check. Google's own AI Overviews cannot be observed with this tool. Section 2.2 therefore records which sources AI-style answers cited when each query was asked as a question. That is a reasonable proxy for what ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode draw on.

### 3.1 Notes on each firm

**Tanmu PMS** (closest like-for-like competitor)
- Services: owner's representation, cost estimating, vendor selection, quality monitoring, energy optimisation, project management. Sectors run from villas to hotels and industrial.
- Strength: community landing pages for Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, Jumeirah Islands, Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills and Dubai South. Its posts target the same questions we do: cost, duration, managing from abroad, owner's representation, contractor checks, renovation mistakes, MEP in renovations.
- Weaknesses: the homepage counters show "0 years / 0M+ sq ft", which are unrendered placeholders. There is a typo in a service URL (`project-mangement-in-dubai.html`), no reviews, no visible licence, and **no structured data on blog posts**. Hotel-brand logos have no case detail. There is no named author.
- How to beat it: author-led E-E-A-T (founder byline, P Chapman Construction record), Article, FAQ and Breadcrumb schema on every article, sourced figures, and keypoints. Their posts have none of these.

**DNA Project Management**
- Five services, including **main contracting and steel contracting** alongside PMC. Claims 17 years, 27+ projects and a largest project of AED 68m.
- Its own article argues that "firms that combine oversight with design or contracting compromise independence", yet DNA itself offers main contracting. That contradiction is exactly the independence argument our `/independence/` page makes.
- Weaknesses: the blog is almost empty, the article runs about 850 words with no byline, sources or FAQ, and there are no reviews or licence details.

**Project Wizards**
- MRICS-led cost and project management for luxury villas (Jumeirah Golf Estates, Al Furjan, Tilal Al Ghaf, Arabian Ranches). It deliberately limits its workload.
- Weaknesses: no blog at all, duplicated copy, typos ("Jumeriah"), and testimonial inconsistencies (one refers to "QS Wizards"). It has nothing for AI to cite beyond the homepage.
- Lesson: its testimonials are its main trust asset. We cannot match that honestly yet, so we lean on the founder's verifiable UK record instead.

**Bauhaus Project Management**
- Renovation, construction, rescue and design management in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. It claims "6+" projects and shows trade testimonials. The posts include a cost-saving case ("car lift inside a home"), overseas owners, permits and timeline, and Ramadan construction.
- Its homepage says it will "assess and **supervise** every supplier". That is the wording our brief forbids us to use. We should not comment on competitors publicly, but our careful wording is a credibility point with sophisticated owners and their lawyers.
- Its project-rescue service overlaps our `/services/stalled-project-rescue/`. It is a live service category in this market.

**DMDC**
- A five-company design-build group with in-house joinery, a renovate-to-resell partnership (Residual.ae) and an investment arm (DMDC Estates). It has a lot of earned press and lists industry awards.
- It is not a client-side competitor. It is the kind of contractor an owner we represent might appoint or compare. Its "approvals and sequence" and "renovation budget" guides overlap our backlog.
- Weakness for owners: the group audits itself ("DMDC Project Management audits the other four companies"). That is the strongest real-world example of why owners want an independent representative. Make the point in general terms only. Never name them in copy.

**Luxedesign**
- A design-build villa contractor. It is #2 for "villa construction dubai" with a service page, and its regular blog covers design cost, renovate vs rebuild, ROI, hidden costs, gated-community rules and pre-build questions.
- Weaknesses: unsupported "award-winning" and "leading" claims, no reviews, an empty CEO section, and no licence shown.
- Topics it covers that we do not yet: renovation ROI, modern renovation, hidden renovation costs, gated-community renovation rules.

**Dar Al Naseeb (DAN)**
- An approvals firm with a very large volume of permit guides, community pages for about 20 communities, authority pages and full LocalBusiness/Service schema. Some of its claims are self-reported: "98% first-time approval", "200+ Palm approvals".
- It publishes a self-served AggregateRating (4.9 from 87) in its homepage schema. Google's review-snippet guidelines do not allow self-serving reviews on a LocalBusiness to earn stars, so this is a risk for them and **not something to copy**.
- Its pattern of batch-publishing many near-identical "2026 complete guide" pages is close to what Google's scaled-content policy targets. Our two-a-week, first-hand approach is the safer long-term strategy.
- Opportunity: DAN describes itself as a DM-registered engineering consultancy. Firms like this are natural **complementary partners** on our side of the line, since the consultant obtains permits and we represent the owner. This is not a recommendation to partner with DAN specifically.

**PRASOON**
- An architect-led owner's representative. It has the best-built content of the eight: FAQPage schema, numbered sources, an author bio and an llms.txt. Its topics are more institutional (escrow, hotel TSAs, PMC RFPs, fees and scope, variation early warning, authority jurisdiction, procurement routes).
- It is the source behind two facts in our brief (Currie & Brown inflation forecast; "no credible benchmark" for owner's-rep fees). AI answers already treat it as an authority on owner's representation.
- Gaps: no reviews, thin service pages, and nothing villa-specific about building work on site. **Our angle, the builder now on the owner's side, is the one thing PRASOON cannot claim.**
