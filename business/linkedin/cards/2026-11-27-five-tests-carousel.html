Five tests
TEST 01

Distress is measured against paid-in equity, not the asking price

We start from the developer's statement of account: what has actually been paid in.

TEST 02

Value is benchmarked to registered DLD transactions

Never portal listings. Asking prices are opinions; the Land Department records what buyers paid.

Five tests
TEST 03

The buyer's all-in cost is normalised

Transfer fees, agency fees, outstanding instalments, service-charge arrears and premiums are all added back.

TEST 04

The commission structure is examined

Inflated commissions routinely fund "discounts". We establish who is paying whom first.

Five tests
TEST 05

The seller's situation is evidenced, not asserted

A payment schedule, a formal notice, a relocation. "Owner needs quick sale" is a claim.

What we decline
  • Developer incentives repackaged as price reductions
  • Open-market resales presented as urgent
  • Deals whose discount disappears once all-in costs are counted
