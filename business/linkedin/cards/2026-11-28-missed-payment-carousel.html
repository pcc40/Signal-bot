Step 1 · The late penalty
On an overdue instalment, commonly
1–2%

a month on the amount outstanding.

Many developers allow a grace period, often 15 to 30 days, to settle without escalation.

A single missed payment caught quickly is usually manageable: pay the instalment and the penalty, and you are current again.

Step 2 · If arrears build
The process runs through the Land Department

The DLD serves notice: typically 30 days to bring the account current.

The DLD may mediate rather than rush to cancellation: restructured payments, a part-payment or a transfer.

A developer cannot seize the unit the moment you slip. There are notices, and there is time.

Step 4 · The exit most miss
Sell before it is cancelled

Paid the developer's minimum threshold, often 30–40%? You can usually assign the contract.

A buyer who clears the arrears as part of the deal solves the developer's problem and yours in one move.

You recover your equity, instead of watching a quarter or more of it go in a retention.
