Prepared 14 November 2026 for sending Fri 20 Nov under Phil's approval of 14 Nov. This part is firms 26–45 (part A covers 1–25). Short style throughout: 55–85 words from "Dear" to "Kind regards,", no link, one closing question. Named-person rule of 14 Nov: where the firm's own site names a founder, director or partner, the email greets that person and the Source line records the person and the page; a personal address is used only where the firm publishes it (Djawad Nawrozzadeh, Julian Walker, Kevin Crowther), otherwise the general address carries the person's first name.
