## 1. The National: stalled villa builds
To: newstips@thenationalnews.com
Subject: Comment offer: the first ten days after a private villa build stalls

Dear News Desk,

When a private villa build in Dubai stalls, owners often act before they have the facts. They stop every payment, threaten termination or bring in a new contractor who cannot price the work fairly.

I can offer comment, or a short piece, on the first ten days. Secure the site and every record. Pause only payments that have not been properly valued. Take UAE legal advice before withholding sums the contract says are due. Record what is built, to what standard and at what cost. Under Dubai Municipality's Administrative Resolution No. 10 of 2019, a new contractor cannot start until a permit is issued in its name.

I founded P Chapman Construction, a UK residential builder, in 2005, so I have seen stalls from the builder's side. I now lead Eastbridge Strategic Partners, a client-side project manager for villas in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Construction Week published my piece on villa budgets last week.

Background: https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/insights/stalled-villa-project-dubai/

## 2. Khaleej Times: the first year after handover
To: news@khaleejtimes.com (cc reporters@khaleejtimes.com)
Subject: Comment offer: what villa owners should test in their first year after handover

Dear News Desk,

The UAE's new Civil Transactions Law applies to contracts made from 1 June 2026. However, most problems in a new villa are not structural. They show up in year one: the first full summer of air conditioning, the first heavy rain, and months of daily use of every bathroom.

I can offer comment, or a short piece, on how owners should use the defects liability period. That means what to test and how to record defects. It also means why the end of that period is the owner's last real leverage over retention.

I founded P Chapman Construction, a UK residential builder, in 2005 and ran it for 21 years. I now lead Eastbridge Strategic Partners, a client-side project manager for villas in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Construction Week published my piece on villa budgets last week. I always recommend UAE legal advice on the contract itself.

Background: https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/insights/defects-liability-period-uae/

## 3. Gulf News Property Weekly: extending a villa
To: propertyweekly@gulfnews.com
Subject: Story idea: extending your villa in Dubai, and what to get in writing first

Dear Editor,

More villa owners are adding space instead of moving. The approvals catch many of them out. In a master community the developer decides first whether an extension is allowed at all, and some developers charge for the added floor area. In 2018 your paper reported Jumeirah Islands owners quoting about AED 300 per sq ft. The authority permit comes next.

I can offer comment, or a short piece, on the order to do things in. Get the developer's answer and any charge in writing, survey the existing house, then design. I can also explain why the junction between old and new is where extension budgets go wrong.

I founded P Chapman Construction, a UK residential builder, in 2005. I now lead Eastbridge Strategic Partners, a client-side project manager for villas in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Construction Week published my piece on villa budgets last week.

Background: https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/insights/villa-extension-dubai/

## 4. MEED: owner-side capacity on private villa projects
To: colin.foreman@meed.com
Subject: Comment offer: why private villa projects in Dubai overrun

Dear Colin,

Dubai's private villa market has grown fast, with plot releases on Dubai Islands, Jumeirah Bay and, in Abu Dhabi, Hudayriyat. Many of these houses are commissioned by families who have never built before. A developer has someone reading every tender, programme and payment the way the contractor does, but a family building one house often does not.

I can offer market comment on where private villa budgets leak: provisional sums, programme drift, unpriced changes and payments that run ahead of progress. I can also cover what owners can do about it. Construction Week published my opinion piece on this last week.

I founded P Chapman Construction, a UK residential builder, in 2005 and ran it for 21 years. I now lead Eastbridge Strategic Partners, a client-side project manager for villas in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
