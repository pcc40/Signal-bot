# Outreach plan, week of 16–20 November 2026 (approved by Phil, 14 Nov)

## Why it changes
228 firms contacted 5–13 Nov; 2 replies (about 1%). One became a warm lead (Fedari: Phil phoned, they offered a partnership and a client introduction). Lessons:
1. Most cold-email replies come from the follow-up, not the first email. Nobody in batches 4–9 has had one yet.
2. Generic inboxes (info@, hello@) rarely reply. Named people do.
3. The reply we got followed a phone call and a concrete reason to talk (referral partnership).
4. Four Office 365 deferrals on 13 Nov suggest our volume is starting to look like bulk mail. Keep to 50 a day, keep it plain, no links in first emails, no tracking.

## What changes from Monday
- **Follow-ups first.** One short follow-up to every firm in batches 4–9 that has not replied or bounced: subject "Re: <original subject>", 25–45 words, quoting nothing, offering one concrete thing:
  - referrers (studios, agencies, inspectors, valuers, brokers, holiday homes, specialists): a referral partnership like Fedari's, with a listing on our Partners page (https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/partners/) for firms we work with;
  - advisers (wealth, law, insurance, conveyancers, mortgage): a one-page owner's checklist they can give clients (https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/guides/villa-owner-checklist/).
  One link only, in the follow-up. Close with a yes/no question.
- **Named people.** New research finds the founder or a named partner on the firm's own site and writes "Dear <first name>". Personal addresses only if the firm publishes them; otherwise the general address with the person's name in the greeting.
- **New segments** (UAE villa studios and agencies are close to exhausted): UK architects, interior designers, estate agents and wealth managers with Dubai or Abu Dhabi clients; UAE developers' after-sales and handover teams; international schools' relocation and admissions teams; chambers and business groups (British, Dutch, German, French, Swiss, Scandinavian business councils in Dubai and Abu Dhabi) for introductions or a talk; engineering/MEP/structural consultancies that do not sell project management.
- **Phone.** Each morning Phil gets a call list of 5 firms that look most promising (with phone numbers from their own sites) to ring after the emails go.
- **Replies stop everything.** No follow-up is sent to anyone who has replied, bounced or asked not to be contacted. Replies are read from Phil's Eastbridge inbox by the Claude Desktop chat each evening (UK time) and written to `business/replies.md`; the morning run reads that file before sending.
