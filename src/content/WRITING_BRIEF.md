# Eastbridge insight articles — writing brief

## Who we are
Eastbridge Strategic Partners (Eastbridge) is an independent, client-side owner's representative and project manager for private clients building or renovating villas in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. The founding partner built and ran a premium residential construction company in the UK for 21 years (bespoke contemporary homes on the Cornish coast — e.g. waterside new-builds, cliff-top houses, cantilevered estuary houses, a development of fifteen beach houses; JCT contracts; extension-of-time and variation disputes, adjudication). The UK company was P Chapman Construction (pchapmanconstruction.com): Owers House (completed in 2014) was featured on Channel 4's Grand Designs (do not add a series, episode or broadcast date), won a RIBA South West Award 2016 and was reported in The Times; LABC Building Excellence Awards 2018 (winner) and 2020 (Highly Commended). Awards belong to the houses and their project teams, not to Philip or Eastbridge. Never name any architect P Chapman Construction built for, anywhere on the site (owner's decision, 31 October 2026); other project-team and photographer credits on the project pages may stay. Eastbridge team: Philip leads every appointment personally, supported by a team of three project managers who attend site and provide cover when he is unavailable (no names, qualifications, nationalities or capacity number). Firm commitments: Philip replies personally within one working day; plot review within five working days of receiving the plot documents; a health check or stalled-project review starts within a week, with the full picture within two to three weeks. Published fees (see src/config.py FEES; confirmed 1 November 2026) are ranges by level of input: Review (plot, design, contractor-quote or stalled-project review, with a written report) AED 9,500–15,000 per review; Part service (cost, contract and payment reviews and monthly reporting, without weekly site presence) a monthly fee agreed in the written scope, with no published minimum; Full service (everything, including site visits and sourcing trips with the owner, travel at cost) typically 1.75–3% of build cost. Every one-off villa is different, so the fee is agreed in a written scope of works after the free call, and work outside that scope is quoted before it starts. Never publish a day rate, an hourly rate or a fixed price, and never a snagging-only fee. Limits of authority: we never instruct the contractor or approve a payment without the owner's written approval. Never mention the company's turnover or revenue. Present this confidently, never apologetically. Eastbridge has not yet completed a UAE project: never claim or imply UAE projects or existing Eastbridge clients, and never volunteer or dwell on the point either. The angle: "we spent two decades as the builder; now we sit on the client's side of the table", so we know where budgets leak, how variations and delay claims are built, and what contractors hope clients won't check.

## Voice
- Firm voice: "we / our / the team". Never say "I". The founder is Philip Chapman, Founding Partner; every article is automatically bylined to him, so name him in the body only where it adds credibility (at most once), e.g. "our founder, Philip Chapman, ran P Chapman Construction for 21 years".
- British English spelling (programme, organise, metre), plain and direct, calm, expert, no hype, no "Best/#1", no emojis, no exclamation marks.
- Write for a wealthy, busy, intelligent owner (often British or European, sometimes living abroad). Short paragraphs. Concrete detail beats adjectives.
- Include at least one genuinely first-hand observation from the builder's side in each article (e.g. "On the contractor's side, the first thing we priced on any job was…"). Keep them general and plausible from 21 years of UK residential building — no client names, no invented project numbers, no awards.
- No invented statistics. Any figure must come from the facts list below (cite the source) or be framed clearly as our own rule of thumb ("in our experience…").

## Compliance — wording rules (important, legal)
Eastbridge is NOT an engineering consultancy office. Under Dubai Law No. 14 of 2025 only DM-registered consultancies may practise or present themselves as engineering consultants or supervision consultants.
- NEVER say Eastbridge supervises construction, is the "supervision consultant", "consultant of record", engineer, or that it obtains/submits permits, signs off, certifies, prepares tender documents or prequalifies contractors.
- SAY: we represent the owner; we work alongside the owner's DM-registered consultant and the contractor; independent client-side site visits and progress reporting; cost and programme control; commercial and contractual advice; we help you compare bids like-for-like and choose; the owner appoints.
- Authority route: say "Dubai Municipality, Trakhees or the Dubai Development Authority, depending on where your plot sits" — do not assert which authority covers a specific community.
- Do not give legal advice; say "take advice from a UAE-qualified lawyer" where relevant.
