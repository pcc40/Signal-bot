A complete kitchen renovation in a Dubai villa costs roughly AED 50,000 to AED 150,000 according to the most recent published guide, and a complete bathroom renovation AED 30,000 to AED 70,000 or more. A large villa kitchen with bespoke joinery and natural stone, or a master bathroom with stone throughout, can go well beyond both. The price is driven far more by cabinetry, stone and whether the plumbing moves than by the finishes people tend to focus on.

This guide sets out the published figures side by side, shows where they disagree, and explains what actually moves the price, from the builder's side.

## Kitchen renovation costs: what the published sources say

| Scope | Property Finder (Oct 2025) | Bayut (Jul 2022) |
|---|---|---|
| Light or cosmetic update | AED 10,000–30,000 (light updates); AED 30,000–50,000 (minor cosmetic upgrade) | AED 2,000–5,500 (small kitchen) |
| Mid-range remodel | AED 50,000–85,000 | AED 4,000–12,000 (modern or traditional) |
| Full luxury renovation | AED 85,000–120,000+ | AED 15,000–30,000 |
| Complete villa kitchen | AED 50,000–150,000 | AED 15,000–100,000 overall range |

Sources: [Property Finder, October 2025](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/kitchen-renovation-dubai/); [Bayut, July 2022](https://bayut.com/mybayut/how-much-cost-renovate-kitchen-dubai).

The two sources are three years apart and their figures are not comparable: Bayut's luxury kitchen tops out below Property Finder's cosmetic upgrade. Property Finder's own figures are not entirely consistent either (its light-update range starts at AED 10,000, its tier table at AED 30,000). For a villa, use the current ranges and treat the lower end with caution.

Property Finder also breaks the budget down by component:

| Component | Property Finder (Oct 2025) |
|---|---|
| Cabinetry | AED 15,000–60,000+ (custom from AED 20,000) |
| Countertops | AED 3,000–15,000 (quartz or granite AED 200–700 per sqm; marble from AED 1,000 per sqm) |
| Appliances | AED 5,000–30,000 |
| Flooring | AED 5,000–20,000 |
| Lighting | AED 1,000–5,000 |
| Labour | AED 5,000–15,000 |

Cabinetry is the largest line by a distance, which is why the choice between modular, semi-custom and fully bespoke joinery matters more than any other decision.

## Bathroom renovation costs: what the published sources say

| Scope | Property Finder (Oct 2025) |
|---|---|
| Minor (tiles, paint, fixtures) | AED 5,000–20,000 |
| Mid-range (new vanity, better lighting and ventilation) | AED 15,000–40,000 |
| Complete (full redesign, plumbing, electrics, luxury fittings) | AED 30,000–70,000+ |
| Small bathroom (4–6 sqm), simple upgrade | AED 8,000–20,000 |
| Medium (6–10 sqm) | AED 20,000–35,000 |
| Large (10+ sqm) | AED 35,000–70,000+ |
| Plumbing or rewiring if the layout changes | AED 5,000–10,000 |

Source: [Property Finder, October 2025](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/bathroom-renovation-dubai/). Property Finder suggests holding a contingency of 10–15% of the total.

A villa typically has four to six bathrooms. Multiply carefully: a villa with a large master bathroom, three medium en-suites and a guest WC can reach a bathroom total of AED 150,000 to well over AED 250,000 on these figures, before any stone or sanitaryware upgrade.

## What really drives the price

**Moving the plumbing.** Keeping the sink, WC and shower where they are is the single biggest saving. Moving a WC means a new drain run, often through a slab, with falls that have to work. Moving a kitchen sink to an island means new drainage and often new ventilation.

**Cabinetry and joinery.** Bespoke joinery is made to measure and has a lead time. Modular cabinetry is quicker and cheaper; semi-custom sits between the two. The joinery choice usually decides whether a kitchen sits at the bottom or top of the published ranges.

**Stone.** Natural stone is priced by the slab, wastes more in cutting and needs careful templating after the cabinets are fitted. It also has to be ordered early.

**Waterproofing and what is behind the tiles.** In an older villa, the cost you cannot see is often the largest risk. On the contractor's side, the first thing we priced on any bathroom refurbishment was what we would find when the old tiles came off. A tanking membrane that has failed, corroded pipework or a shower tray with no proper fall all become extras once the room is stripped. A quick inspection or a small opening-up before the price is agreed turns a contractor's guess into a number.

**Ventilation and electrics.** New extraction, underfloor heating, mirror lighting and sockets for appliances all need circuits and routes. On a full renovation they belong in the price from the start.

## Hidden costs owners forget

- **Approvals.** In a master community, alterations usually need the developer's consent first. Emaar Community Management says any alteration to the villa must be approved by Emaar ([Emaar Community Management](https://www.ecm.ae/en/communities/arabian-ranches/lifestyle/faqs/home-modifications-arabian-ranches/)); Nakheel says an NOC is needed before any alterations ([Nakheel](https://www.nakheel.com/en/contact-us)). Contractors also need community access permits.
- **Living without a kitchen.** Three to eight weeks without a kitchen is Property Finder's estimate for the works alone. Plan where the family will cook and eat.
- **Protection and making good.** Floors, stairs and adjoining rooms need protecting, and redecorating next to the new room is easy to leave out of the price.
- **Ordering too late.** Late decisions on stone, sanitaryware or appliances stop the site and are then priced as delays.

## How to get a fair price

1. **Decide the layout first,** including whether any plumbing moves.
2. **Write a scope with the specification** for cabinetry, tops, appliances, tiles and sanitaryware, or provisional sums with a stated allowance for each.
3. **Ask every contractor to price the same scope line by line,** so you can compare like for like. Our guide to [choosing a villa contractor](/insights/how-to-choose-a-villa-contractor-dubai/) has the questions to ask.
4. **Hold a contingency** that matches the age of the house.
5. **Pay against progress** you have seen, and keep a retention until snags are closed. Our [snagging checklist](/insights/villa-snagging-checklist-dubai/) covers kitchens and bathrooms room by room.
