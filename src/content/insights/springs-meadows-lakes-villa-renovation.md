The Springs, The Meadows and The Lakes are Emaar communities within Emirates Living, and they share one rulebook. Emaar's approval comes before any alteration, through a home modification NOC, and Emaar's guidance for Emirates Living says changes to the exterior of the villa are not permitted. A good renovation here works with that: the value is made inside the house, in the garden within the rules, and in renewing what has aged.

## What are the three communities?

Emaar Community Management describes them like this ([Emaar Community Management](https://www.ecm.ae/en/communities/emirates-living/discover/communities-emirates-living)):

| Community | Homes | Type |
|---|---|---|
| The Springs | 4,856 homes across 14 sub-communities | Two- to four-bedroom townhouses, plus five-bedroom detached villas |
| The Meadows | Nine sub-communities | Two-storey detached villas, three to seven bedrooms, with large gardens |
| The Lakes | Nearly 1,000 homes across five sub-communities (Deema, Forat, Maeen, Zulal and Ghadeer) | Detached villas, three to five bedrooms, on generous plots |

The Meadows was launched by Emaar in 2004 ([Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/community-guides/dubai/meadows)), and the neighbouring communities are of a similar generation. Some houses have been renewed; many still have original services, roofs and wet areas.

## What does Emaar allow?

Emaar Community Management's Emirates Living guidance is short: any alteration to the villa has to be approved by Emaar, applied for as a home modification NOC through the community portal; changes to the exterior of the villa are not permitted; and lights may not be fitted on top of boundary walls or on their community-facing side ([Emaar Community Management](https://www.ecm.ae/en/communities/emirates-living/lifestyle/faqs/home-modifications-emirates-living)).

That shapes what a renovation can be. Kitchens, bathrooms, internal layout, services, finishes and joinery are where most of the work goes, each subject to Emaar's NOC and, where the scope needs one, an authority permit through a registered consultant. Anything that would change the outside of the house should be raised with Emaar in writing before it is designed. Our guide to the [Emaar community process](/insights/emaar-community-villa-renovation/) sets out the full sequence, from NOC to contractor entry permit, and our guide to [changing a villa's exterior](/insights/villa-exterior-changes-dubai/) compares Emaar's rules with other developers'.

## Townhouses in The Springs: what is different?

Most homes in The Springs are townhouses, and a townhouse renovation is not a small villa renovation. The walls you share with your neighbours change the job:

- **Structure.** Removing or opening walls near the party wall needs a registered structural engineer's view, in writing, before anyone prices it. Our guide to [changing a villa's layout](/insights/changing-villa-layout-dubai-approvals/) explains who approves what.
- **Neighbours.** Noise, dust and vibration travel. Agree working hours and protection with the contractor, within the community's rules, and tell the neighbours before you start.
- **Waterproofing and junctions.** Roofs and terraces that meet the next house are where water gets in. Any roof or wet-area work should be detailed at the junctions, not just across the middle.
- **Access.** Narrow fronts mean skips, deliveries and storage need planning before the strip-out, not on the first morning.

## Detached villas in The Meadows and The Lakes

The larger villas in The Meadows and The Lakes give more freedom inside, and bigger gardens to work with. The risk shifts to what twenty years have done to the house. Before design, survey and open up: air-conditioning, wiring and boards, water pipes, drainage, roof waterproofing and wet areas. Our guide to [rewiring and replumbing an older Dubai villa](/insights/older-villa-mep-renovation/) sets out what to check, and our [villa renovation cost guide](/insights/villa-renovation-cost-dubai/) covers the budget, including the hidden costs older villas tend to have.

In the garden, pools, pergolas, shade structures and landscaping all need Emaar's approval. Keep them inside the guidelines and design them early, because garden work is often the last package and the first to be squeezed.

## A view from the builder's side

On repeated house types, like a street of townhouses or a cluster of similar villas, contractors often price from the last job they did nearby. That is efficient until the house in front of them turns out to be different: an earlier owner's alteration, a different services route, a wall that is not where the standard drawing says it is. On our side of the table, the cheapest protection was always the same: open up a little before you price a lot, and do not let anyone treat your house as the one next door.
