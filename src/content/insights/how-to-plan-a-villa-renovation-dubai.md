To plan a villa renovation in Dubai, work in this order: decide whether to renovate or rebuild, learn what your community allows, survey the existing villa, set a budget and a programme you can defend, design and get the master developer's NOC and any authority permit, then choose the contractor and agree the contract. Only then should anyone start on site. Most renovations that overrun went wrong in the first half of that list, not the second.

This page is our renovation hub. Each step below has a short summary of what matters and links to the guide or tool that covers it in full.

## Step 1: Decide whether to renovate or rebuild

Start with what you want the villa to do: more bedrooms, a better layout, a modern kitchen and services, a pool, a different look. Then ask honestly whether the existing house can get you there. A renovation keeps the old structure, slab levels and ceiling heights; a rebuild resets them, but takes far longer and costs more per square foot.

Our [renovate or rebuild guide](/insights/renovate-or-rebuild-villa-dubai/) compares the two with sourced rates and a worked example, including the months the family spends out of the house. The [renovate-or-rebuild tool](/tools/renovate-or-rebuild/) runs the same comparison on your own villa size and specification. When the brief keeps growing towards new structure, new services and a new envelope, it is usually time to look at the rebuild figures properly.

If the plan includes more space, our guide to [extending a Dubai villa](/insights/villa-extension-dubai/) covers developer consent, built-up area limits and what extensions really cost. For an interior-led project, see [villa fit-out in Dubai](/insights/villa-fit-out-dubai/).

## Step 2: Learn what your community allows

In Dubai's master-planned communities, the master developer decides what can change before any authority sees the drawings. Nakheel says an NOC from its Urban Planning team is needed before any alteration ([Nakheel](https://www.nakheel.com/en/contact-us)). In Arabian Ranches, Emaar Community Management says any alteration needs Emaar's approval through a home modification NOC, and external changes that alter the look of the villas are not permitted ([Emaar Community Management](https://www.ecm.ae/en/communities/arabian-ranches/lifestyle/faqs/home-modifications-arabian-ranches/)).

That makes your community the second question, not an afterthought. Our area guides set out the master developer, the approval route and the typical villa stock:

- [Palm Jumeirah](/areas/palm-jumeirah/), with our detailed guide to [renovating a Palm Jumeirah villa](/insights/palm-jumeirah-villa-renovation/): older frond villas, salt exposure, Nakheel's site requirements.
- [Jumeirah Islands](/areas/jumeirah-islands/): a Nakheel community where owners have reported charges for added floor area and penalties for unapproved work ([Gulf News, 2018](https://gulfnews.com/news/gulf/uae/jumeirah-islands-owners-prevented-from-modifying-villas-without-approval-1.1172089)).
- [Arabian Ranches](/areas/arabian-ranches/): an Emaar community with sub-community home modification guidelines. Our guide to [renovating in an Emaar community](/insights/emaar-community-villa-renovation/) sets out the sequence.
- [Emirates Hills](/areas/emirates-hills/) (Emaar) and [Dubai Hills Estate](/areas/dubai-hills-estate/) (Emaar with Meraas): each guide sets out the master developer's approval route.

If you are unsure whether a piece of work needs approval, assume it does until the master developer has said otherwise in writing.

## Step 3: Survey the villa before anything is designed

An older villa hides its condition behind finishes. Air-conditioning, wiring, plumbing, drainage and waterproofing are the usual unknowns, and structure sometimes is too. A condition survey arranged through the right specialists, with some opening-up of walls, ceilings and wet areas, answers those questions while answers are still cheap. Our article on [rewiring and replumbing an older Dubai villa](/insights/older-villa-mep-renovation/) covers the services survey in detail.

Our guide to [villa renovation mistakes](/insights/villa-renovation-mistakes-dubai/) puts skipping this step near the top of the list, for a simple reason: whatever the survey does not find, the strip-out will, and by then the contractor is on site and pricing the answer.

## Step 4: Set a budget for the whole project

Published renovation figures disagree. [Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/villa-renovation-dubai/) runs from AED 250,000–400,000 for a three-bedroom townhouse refresh to AED 1.5–3.5 million or more for structural work on a luxury villa, while [a Dubai renovation contractor's 2026 guide](https://smartrenovation.ae/villa-renovation-in-dubai-complete-2026-guide-to-costs-timeline-permits/) puts mid-range work at AED 400,000–800,000 and luxury from AED 1 million. Both are mostly the contractor's price.

A real budget adds design fees, approval charges, any master-developer charge for added area, external works, furniture, the cost of living elsewhere and a contingency. Engel & Völkers says a contingency of 10–15% of total project cost is widely recommended ([Engel & Völkers](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en/resources/construction-cost-dubai)); on an older villa with original services we would plan at the upper end. Our [villa renovation cost guide](/insights/villa-renovation-cost-dubai/) works through a 6,000 sq ft example, and the [villa cost calculator](/tools/villa-build-cost-calculator/) gives a sourced range for your own size and specification.

## Step 5: Build a realistic programme

On site, published guides put a smaller interior refresh at about two to three months and structural work on a luxury villa at 150–210 days. Survey, design, approvals and tender come before that. Our [villa renovation timeline](/insights/villa-renovation-timeline-dubai/) breaks the whole journey into phases with our planning allowances, and explains what stretches a Dubai programme: the summer midday break from 15 June to 15 September ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/midday-outdoor-work-ban-returns-across-uae-for-22nd-year-1.500567381)), shorter working hours in Ramadan, NOC comments and long-lead imports.

If time out of the house has a cost, the [delay cost estimator](/tools/delay-cost-estimator/) shows what each month of slippage adds in rent and finance.

## Step 6: Design, then get the NOC and any permit

The design should be priced as it develops, so that what goes for approval is something you can afford. Your DM-registered consultant then submits to the master developer and, with the NOC in hand, applies for the permit where the scope needs one, to Dubai Municipality, Trakhees or the Dubai Development Authority, depending on where your plot sits. Service-authority NOCs are gathered through Dubai's unified permit window ([Dubai Municipality](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-launches-single-window-for-building-permits/)), and the Build in Dubai platform covers alterations as well as new builds ([Build in Dubai](https://buildindubai.gov.ae/)).

Our [Nakheel NOC guide](/insights/nakheel-noc-villa-renovation/) explains what triggers an NOC, what Nakheel asks for and the site requirements that follow. Our guide to [which Dubai authority covers my villa](/insights/which-dubai-authority-covers-my-villa/) explains how to confirm the permit route for your plot, and the [Dubai villa building permit guide](/insights/dubai-municipality-villa-building-permit/) covers that stage step by step.

## Step 7: Choose the contractor and agree the contract

Renovation is a different skill from building new. Ask bidders about comparable renovations, how they handle discoveries after strip-out and how they price provisional sums. Give every bidder the same survey findings and approved drawings, then compare the exclusions as carefully as the totals. Our guide on [how to choose a villa contractor in Dubai](/insights/how-to-choose-a-villa-contractor-dubai/) lists the questions to ask, and an independent [contractor quote review](/services/consultant-and-contractor-selection/#quote-review) sets bids side by side on the same basis.

The contract should tie the start date to approvals in hand, set out how changes are priced and agreed, and link payments to work done. Our guide to [villa construction contracts in the UAE](/insights/villa-construction-contracts-uae/) explains the main forms and clauses; take advice from a UAE-qualified lawyer before you sign.

Our guide to [choosing a villa renovation company](/insights/how-to-choose-villa-renovation-company-dubai/) sets out ten checks to make before you sign, and [kitchen and bathroom renovation costs](/insights/villa-kitchen-bathroom-renovation-cost-dubai/) shows what the two most expensive rooms usually cost.

## Step 8: Run the works with your hands on the controls

Three things keep a renovation under control once it starts. First, finishes chosen and long-lead items ordered against the programme, not after the strip-out; our guide to [sourcing materials abroad](/insights/sourcing-materials-abroad-dubai-villa/) covers lead times, shipping and customs. Second, every change agreed in writing, with its price and its effect on the programme, before the work is done. Third, payments that follow progress rather than run ahead of it; the [stage payment planner](/tools/stage-payment-planner/) shows how a schedule should track the work.

On the contractor's side, we knew within the first few weeks of a renovation which clients would end up paying for changes. It was rarely the ones with the most ambitious briefs. It was the ones who agreed things on site, by word of mouth, and found out the price at the next valuation. Our founder, Philip Chapman, spent 21 years running P Chapman Construction in the UK, and the habit of writing every change down before it is built is still the single most valuable one an owner can have.

## Step 9: Snag it properly before you accept it

Handover is the last point at which you have real leverage. Test the air-conditioning, water pressure and hot water, drainage, electrics and lighting controls, glazing and doors, and look closely at finishes in good light. Our [villa snagging guide](/insights/villa-snagging-checklist-dubai/) goes room by room, and the [printable snagging checklist](/guides/villa-snagging-checklist/) adds a severity column and a snag log. Keep the retention, if the contract has one, until the snags are closed.
