The payment planner, delay estimator and renovate/rebuild outputs were reviewed. This is not exhaustive testing of every possible input combination.

No website code, pricing or external account configuration was changed. No advertising was launched. Mailbox delivery, Netlify form handling and CRM arrival remain untested end to end. No authenticated Search Console, GA4 or Ads account was available for performance verification. The PageSpeed Insights API returned HTTP429, so there is no verified Lighthouse score or Core Web Vitals result. The browser review was desktop; responsive CSS was inspected, but this is not a completed real-phone visual audit or formal accessibility certification.

WHAT HAS IMPROVED

1. A more concrete founder-led proposition.
The updated site describes drawing, quote, payment and variation review, regular site attendance and sourcing trips. The About page now states that three project managers provide support/cover. These details give an owner a more tangible picture than generic promises about delivering quality.

2. The client-side role is explained clearly.
The distinction between owner representation, the registered consultant and the contractor is consistently useful. Independence, direct appointment/payment and the limits of authority are explained. Preserve this rather than copying competitors' broader technical or certification promises.

3. Real deliverables are shown.
The cost-control page lists reporting, payment recommendations, registers and forecasts; the sourcing page explains sample records, lead times and delivery checks. This helps a buyer understand what the fee buys. A sanitised sample report would be a useful next proof asset, without inventing a past appointment.

4. Stronger resources and internal journeys.
The guides, calculators and community-specific pages now give owners useful reasons to engage before they need full management. The free estimate-review route is a good low-friction offer. The service-specific enquiry link tested carries its service parameter, and its WhatsApp link substitutes the correct topic rather than leaving a template placeholder.

5. More careful attribution.
UK projects are labelled as P Chapman Construction contracting work. The Island E example is explicitly hypothetical and separates published inputs, planning assumptions and invented events. That honesty strengthens the site; do not remove the disclosures to make the portfolio look larger.

6. Better fee visibility.
The live offer has changed: reviews are now AED9,500–15,000 per review; part service is from AED28,000/month; full service is typically 1.75–3% of build cost. A fixed AED9,500 plot-review ad from an earlier brief no longer accurately describes the current range. Update campaign copy before use.

PRIORITY 1: CLARIFY THE FEE MODELS BEFORE PAID TRAFFIC

Confirmed page: https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/fees/
The page's worked example shows full service at approximately AED90,000–153,000 over the whole project, on a stated construction figure of AED5.115m. It separately prices part service, without weekly site attendance, from AED28,000/month.

Twelve months at the part-service minimum is AED336,000, substantially more than the full-service example. A prospect can reasonably ask why the option described as more comprehensive is cheaper. Different appointment durations and resource requirements may explain it, but the page does not make that comparison clear enough.

This is a commercial explanation problem, not necessarily a mathematical mistake. Phil should decide the intended relationship between the models. Claude must not invent a minimum, increase prices or create a new package to resolve it.

Recommended change once the business terms are decided:
- Show the same illustrative project and appointment duration under each applicable model.
- Define the percentage base: accepted tender, approved budget, final construction cost or another agreed basis; clarify VAT, pool, landscaping, direct purchases and exclusions.
- State included duration, named team, attendance frequency, deliverables and treatment of overruns/extra scope.
- If the models are alternatives for different project types rather than ascending packages, make that explicit in the headings and comparison.
- Reconcile the example across the fee article, FAQs, translated pages, schema and llms.txt.

Acceptance: an owner can compare scope, duration and total fee without needing to guess which headline is comparable.

PRIORITY 1: FIX AND VERIFY CONVERSION MEASUREMENT

Confirmed sampled pages: home, contact, guides and calculator HTML.
The site loads GA4 measurement ID G-120XT9N6XB. The general enquiry script fires generate_lead with form='brief' in the submit handler, after client validation but before server acceptance. Checklist requests also use generate_lead, with a different form parameter. WhatsApp clicks use a contact event. No Ads AW- ID was found in sampled source; that does not rule out GA4 imports or another account-side setup.

Why it matters: a failed delivery can be recorded as an enquiry; a blanket generate_lead conversion can combine owner enquiries and checklist requests. The parameters make separation possible, but the account configuration must actually use them. I cannot confirm from public HTML whether those events are currently imported as Ads conversions.

The calculator has its own estimate form. I did not find a dedicated successful-estimate conversion event in its page source. Verify it separately instead of assuming the general enquiry handler covers it.

Required implementation:
- Use separate enquiry_success, estimate_request_success, checklist_request_success and newsletter signals after confirmed acceptance.
- Keep phone/WhatsApp clicks as secondary interactions until an actual conversation can be attributed.
- Configure a single primary conversion source for a genuine enquiry; avoid counting the same action via both an Ads tag and GA4 import.
- Capture eligible campaign parameters/click IDs through the journey and form, respecting consent. No gclid or utm_source handling was found in the sampled contact page source; verify any host/backend handling before adding a duplicate mechanism.
- Track qualified leads, held consultations, paid reviews and appointments separately in the CRM.
- Use an explicitly labelled test submission in an authorised workflow to verify host receipt, mailbox delivery, CRM entry and conversion deduplication. This audit did not send that submission.

Acceptance: one received test enquiry creates one intended primary conversion; a failed submission, newsletter signup or checklist request does not create that primary conversion.

PRIORITY 1: REVIEW THE ACTUAL CONSENT IMPLEMENTATION

The privacy notice now discloses Analytics and calculator submissions. That is an improvement. In the sampled HTML, GA4 configuration loads directly; no analytics consent-default flow was found, and no cookie-choice interface was visible in the browser sessions reviewed.

PRIORITY 2: MAKE COST-TO-COMPLETE DEFINITIONS UNAMBIGUOUS

Confirmed page: https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/services/cost-and-programme-control/
The illustrative report describes forecast cost to complete as committed plus pending changes and priced risks. Another description combines paid, committed and estimates of what remains. Depending on whether committed means total contract commitments or only unpaid future commitments, this can double-count paid work or confuse forecast final cost with remaining cost.

Use one consistent accounting vocabulary, for example:
- Paid to date: cash actually paid.
- Remaining committed cost: committed expenditure not yet paid, with retention/recovery treatment stated.
- Remaining uncommitted cost: forecast work still to procure.
- Risk allowance: explicit remaining risks/contingency, with no duplicate allowance.
- Forecast cost to complete: the remaining amounts, excluding paid-to-date.
- Forecast final cost: paid-to-date plus forecast cost to complete.

Adapt that model to the actual reporting convention; do not mechanically add the whole contract value to paid-to-date. Explain valuation/certification, outstanding liabilities, advance recovery and retention where they affect the figures. This is especially important for a firm selling commercial scrutiny.

PRIORITY 2: RECONCILE THE TWO RENOVATION ESTIMATING JOURNEYS

Confirmed pages:
https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/tools/villa-build-cost-calculator/
https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/tools/renovate-or-rebuild/

For mid-range renovation, the main calculator uses whole-villa works bands of AED400,000–800,000 and disables area input. The renovate/rebuild tool uses area-based works rates; at 6,000 sq ft its works range is AED1.5m–2.7m. The pages explicitly disclose this difference and limitations. That is not a hidden arithmetic bug.

Nevertheless, two resources on the same consultancy site can give markedly different answers to an owner's apparently similar question. A footnote explaining conflicting source methodologies does not fully resolve the decision problem.

Recommendation: use a common scope/size framework, or rename the band-based option as a typical interior-refresh example and the area-based option as a whole-villa refurbishment estimate. Put the distinction before the result. Do not replace the rates with invented market benchmarks. For large bespoke villas, consider prioritising the area-based route with explicit condition/scope limitations and your professional review.

Recheck landscaping, FF&E, basement and approvals assumptions against actual comparable scopes/quotes as evidence becomes available. The site honestly labels weaker secondary sources; retain that transparency. A sourced range is not automatically appropriate for every luxury waterfront villa. Keep the all-in exclusions visible near the headline total.

The new-build unit conversion tested retained the same AED total when switching sq ft to m². Switching to renovation correctly displayed the whole-villa limitation and disabled the unused area field. Preserve those behaviours.

PRIORITY 2: SIMPLIFY THE ENQUIRY FORM'S PRESENTATION

Keep name, email and project summary visible with preferred reply method. Group the optional service, location, stage, size and budget fields under a clearly labelled optional details section, or test a short initial form against the full one. Preserve context prefill from service pages. Avoid adding required document uploads at first contact.

The empty-form test correctly blocked submission and showed useful errors. Preserve validation and the separate, unchecked marketing subscription choice. Do not mistake this result for verified successful email delivery.

SEO AND AI SEARCH ASSESSMENT

The site has the technical ingredients for discovery: working HTML, indexable text, internal links, language alternates, relevant service/community topics, authorship and useful resources. These are meaningful strengths.

However, this audit's public exact-domain/site searches did not surface the new site. Search tools are incomplete, and that is not proof of no Google indexation. Confirm in Search Console: domain ownership, sitemap processing, indexing reports, representative URL Inspection and search queries/impressions. Technical eligibility does not guarantee Google will index or rank a page.

Likely opportunity, not a forecast: specific owner questions about client-side villa fees, contractor-quote reviews, independent villa management and Island E plot decisions are more aligned with the site than broad searches such as project management Dubai. Do not claim a first-page date without demand, competition, authority and Search Console evidence.

For Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Google states that standard SEO practices apply, that pages need to be indexed and snippet-eligible, and that no special AI markup or AI text file is required. llms.txt and bot permissions cannot substitute for useful content or external credibility. Other AI/search services have their own discovery and retrieval systems; this review did not measure citation frequency across them.

Next authority work:
- Obtain genuine, consented testimonials/references with the correct company, role and project attribution.
- Publish a real redacted output sample and, when available, a real UAE appointment case study with verifiable role/outcomes.
- Use consistent company/founder identifiers on legitimate professional profiles and relevant directories; only create a Business Profile if the real operating arrangement satisfies Google's eligibility requirements.
- Seek editorial mentions or useful tool embeds from actual professional partners. Avoid purchased bulk links, fabricated reviews and mass location pages.
- Record the source date behind numerical claims and review market-sensitive facts on a maintained schedule.
- Measure organic enquiries and qualified outcomes alongside impressions; rankings alone do not establish commercial performance.
