# Eastbridge website: review fixes (brief of 31 October 2026)

Status: the changes the existing facts support are in place and checked. Items that depend on the owner are listed in section 4 and are **not** complete. The site is not declared launch-ready.

## 1. Summary of completed changes, by brief section

**1. Island E repositioned.** The page is now "How we would manage an Island E villa: a worked project example". A disclosure that the project is hypothetical, and is neither a completed Eastbridge appointment nor a promise of savings, comes before any numbers on the case hub, the worked-example page, cards, metadata and Article JSON-LD. Invented events use conditional wording. Published plot information, planning assumptions and invented events sit in separate columns, and draft controls are marked draft. Every simulated figure in tables, events, the sample report and chart labels carries "Modelled". The simulated savings and under-budget tiles are gone from the homepage, why-page and case hub, replaced by the outputs (budget structure, cash-flow planning, tender comparison, programme review). The URL is unchanged. Owers House is now a genuine UK case study, credited to P Chapman Construction as main contractor, and leads the case hub. *Purpose: no reader should take a model for a UAE track record.*

**2. Worked budget made complete and transparent.** The budget covers plot, DLD registration fee (2% + AED 20, sourced), construction, basement, pool and landscape, design fees, approvals, contingency and 5% VAT only where it applies (bare land exempt). It is labelled a "subtotal of stated allowances" (AED 34.37m). A separate list covers items awaiting quotation: the Eastbridge fee, the seller's DLD share, developer charges, VAT on approvals, escalation and furniture. The price base is October 2026, the construction period is 19 months, and escalation is not included. Cash flow, chart, KPIs, sample report and final account all come from one data set, with build-time assertions. Undrawn contingency is no longer presented as a saving, and modelled cost avoidance is not totalled or offered as a return on the fee. *Purpose: the figures add up and say what they leave out.*

**3. UAE delivery arrangements clarified.** /approach/ has a new section, "How an appointment works in the UAE". It covers who leads, site attendance and reporting agreed in the written scope, specialists appointed and paid directly by the owner, and the boundaries with statutory design, submissions, supervision and legal advice. The same facts appear on /about/, the owner's-representative service page and /faq/. No staff, partners, insurance or UAE projects are named. Cover and capacity are not described until the owner confirms them (section 4). *Purpose: explain how the work is delivered without inventing a team.*

**4. First purchase and enquiry made clearer.** /fees/ now runs free call, then fixed-fee first review (plot review or project health check), then appointment. Each review has its scope, deliverables, exclusions and how the fee is set. The repeated "Typical fee: quoted on request" column has been replaced. Fee figures stay hidden (`PUBLISH_FEES = False`). The site-wide primary CTA reads "Arrange a free 30-minute call" and does not imply instant booking. On the contact form, Required and Optional are marked clearly, phone becomes required when Phone call or WhatsApp is chosen, and the phone number and email address are preserved. The reply time is now worded as an aim ("Philip aims to reply personally within one working day") until the owner confirms it. *Purpose: an owner knows what the first step is and what happens next.*

**5. Homepage shortened and tone corrected.** The homepage is down from 15 sections to 7 and from about 1,654 to 899 words, in the order the brief asks for. The duplicated service grid, credentials strip, testimonials and repeated "21 years" and "paid only by the owner" lines have gone. Absolute promises have been removed site-wide: on time, on budget, "pays for itself", "an avoided variation typically exceeds the fee", "only one of them is paid to protect your money" and similar. Copy now describes controls, and consultants' and contractors' duties are recognised. *Purpose: persuasive without guarantees.*

**6. Calculator localisation and renovation behaviour fixed.** All text generated by calc.js now comes from a translatable message list in the page, and calc.js contains no English text. The 61 messages are translated in ar, ru, zh and hi. Numbers use Latin digits, and amounts are isolated on RTL pages. Areas are held in sq ft, so switching units keeps the area and the estimate. Basic and mid-range renovation are whole-villa scope examples: the area input is disabled with an explanation. Luxury and ultra-luxury renovation show a starting figure only. A renovation result links to a project-specific assessment. Totals equal the sum of the lines shown, and pool and garden choices are kept per mode. *Purpose: no English on translated calculators, and no false precision.*

**7. Privacy and marketing aligned.** The privacy notice describes the systems actually used: Netlify Forms, optional confirmation emails, an optional Google Sheets log and manual unsubscribe. The enquiry and checklist forms each have a separate, unticked, optional `marketing_opt_in` box, and both submit without it. `submission-created.js` records the choice in the sheet row, the log line and the lead alert, sends no marketing to people who did not opt in, and only emails the checklist to people who opted in. The standalone notes form still works. No automation is claimed and no tracking has been added. The final legal wording is flagged for review. *Purpose: consent is a real, recorded choice.*

**8. Sources and attribution strengthened.** Evidence links now sit beside each award and press claim: RIBA South West 2016 (Owers House, via John Pardey Architects), LABC South West 2018 (Old Station Master's House), LABC South West 2020 Highly Commended (Polgreen Manor, CSA Architects). Awards are worded as belonging to the house and its team. Architects and photographers are credited from P Chapman Construction's project pages. Unsupported dates and claims have been removed: the Owers House completion date, Morvoren "delivered to budget" and the Three Mile Beach scope. The Grand Designs line no longer names a property. The KPMG 2015 figures are off the homepage and labelled with year and scope where they remain. Regulatory statements have been rechecked against Dubai Municipality, Dubai Media Office, DMT and WAM sources. *Purpose: every claim can be checked.*

**9. Technical foundation preserved, plus preview indexing.** Routes, the 301 for the old owner's-rep URL, canonicals to https://eastbridgestrategicpartners.com/, hreflang, sitemap, responsive images, accessible labels and reduced-motion handling are unchanged. New in this round: `INDEXABLE` in `src/config.py` reads the `EB_INDEXABLE` environment variable and defaults to True. `EB_INDEXABLE=0 python3 build.py` gives a preview build with noindex on every page and `Disallow: /` in robots.txt, without touching canonicals or the domain. A normal build stays indexable.

**Translations (this round).** 644 new or changed English segments were translated into Arabic, Russian, Chinese and Hindi. Each of the 48 chunks passed `tools/i18n_check.py` with 0 problems. They were merged into `src/i18n/<lang>.json`, and the parts and todo files were deleted. 0 segments are untranslated in any language. In Chinese, the calculator's compact money formats now read "AED {n}百万" and "AED {n}千" instead of the English "m"/"k".

Limitations. These items are not established by the checks above:
- **Email delivery is unverified.** No live enquiry was sent and no real address was subscribed. Confirmation emails, lead alerts and the Google Sheets log depend on Netlify environment variables (`EMAIL_API_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`, `SHEET_WEBHOOK_URL`) and a verified sending domain. None of these were tested end to end, and the drag-and-drop package does not deploy the function at all.
- **Domain and mailbox are not verified.** The site and its canonicals assume eastbridgestrategicpartners.com and philip@eastbridgestrategicpartners.com. DNS, hosting on that domain and the mailbox itself were not checked.
- **Real-device mobile is unverified.** The responsive checks used Playwright Chromium emulation at 360, 390 and 768 px. No physical iOS or Android device was tested, and Safari/WebKit and Firefox were not run.
- **Video playback is unverified.** HANDOFF.md records that Vimeo video 219974514 (also the Quay House film) refused embedding on the site's domain. The Quay House film may not play on eastbridgestrategicpartners.com until the Vimeo embed setting allows it.
- **Rankings, search or AI-assistant visibility, page-speed performance and WCAG conformance have not been measured or established.** The contrast script and accessible-label checks are partial indicators only.
- **Translation quality** has been checked mechanically (structure, links, placeholders, numbers, nothing left in English). It has not been reviewed by a native speaker.
- **Legal wording** has not been reviewed. The privacy notice, consent text and footer notes still need owner or legal review (section 4).
